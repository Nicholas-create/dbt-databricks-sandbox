# GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol, announced 17 Nov 2026

**Source:** https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol/ (charts; read 19 Nov 2026)

**Entered:** 19 Nov 2026.

Values from each chart's embedded data: each model's best score across effort levels, with
that effort recorded.

**Entered:** GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol, plus GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol where this page
gives them a result we didn't have.

**Benchmark mapping:**

- Agents' Last Exam v1.1 = agents-last-exam-v1-1 (GPT-6 Astra 73.1% here vs 59.3% on v1;
  Gemini 4 Carbon 77.4% on both this chart and Google's card).
- FrontierCode 2.0 = frontiercode-2 (Opus 5.5 45.5% and Fable 5.2 49.1% match Anthropic's pages).
- AutomationBench 1.0.6 = automationbench (GPT-6 Astra 41.4% and GPT-6.1 Sol 36.1% match).
- Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 = terminal-bench-science (GPT-6 Astra 68.1%, GPT-6.1 Sol 57.0% match).
- New: BenchCAD 2.1 (Core), a variant in the benchcad family (coding).
- New, hidden `internal` category: GDPval v2 (labelled "Internal"), SecureSWE ("an internal
  benchmark"), LifeVal and PatchBench (scored only on OpenAI models so far, so they can't rank
  models across labs; move them to a visible category if other labs start reporting them).

**Newest page wins:** ExploitBench (Jun–Aug 2026), GPT-6 Astra: 39.0% on the GPT-6 Astra page,
31.5% here.
